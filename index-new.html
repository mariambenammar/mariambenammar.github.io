What I'm building

A children's EQ universe, powered by technology.

Children are growing up surrounded by screens but without tools for their biggest feelings. I'm building a universe of stories, an animated series, games and an interactive app that teaches children sublimation: turning anger and pain into art and healthy expression, instead of holding it inside or letting it out in ways that hurt.

Why me

Why I'm building it.

This is my life's work. I want every child to grow up somewhere safe and healthy, especially the ones who don't yet have words for what they feel.

I believe protection begins with the child. A child who knows what is safe, what is not, and how to say it, is a child who can be heard. It all starts with self-expression. Give children healthy tools, wrapped in play and laughter, and they will carry them for life.

Every decision, every partnership, every word, every action answers to one question: is this good for a child's mind?
